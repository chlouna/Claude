# Infos Michelin Truckfly : Profil Trucker (traductions)

## EN
**Michelin Truckfly News**
**Trucker Profile: drivers no longer ride alone**

Last month, we introduced the Trucker Profile: a personalised profile, reviews of establishments, favourite stops and other drivers' profiles.

This month, we focus on what lies at its heart: the community. With its social features, Michelin Truckfly becomes a true network between drivers.

Even alone in their cab, they stay connected to their colleagues.

**How it works:**
1. Add friends and accept their invitations in a single tap.
2. See where they stop throughout the day: fuel station, truck stop, parking area.
3. Benefit from their recommendations, highlighted in the app.
