1. Christmas and New Year: where will you spend your stop?


On 25 December and 1 January, trucks over 7.5 t are stopped for 24 hours in France. Better pick your truck stop in advance!


France (over 7.5 t, whole road network)

  • From Thursday 24 December 10 pm to Friday 25 December 10 pm
  • From Thursday 31 December 10 pm to Friday 1 January 10 pm
  • And at the weekend, as usual, from Saturday 10 pm to Sunday 10 pm


Italy (over 7.5 t, outside built-up areas, 9 am to 10 pm)

  • Sundays 6, 13, 20 and 27 December
  • Tuesday 8, Friday 25 and Saturday 26 December
  • From 25 to 27 December, that’s 3 days in a row


Spain

  • No general ban: check local restrictions before you set off.


Good habit: on Michelin Truckfly, find truck stops with showers and a restaurant along your route now, and read recent reviews to see what’s open over the holidays.
