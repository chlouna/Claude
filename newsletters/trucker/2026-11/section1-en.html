1. Festive season: the days you can’t drive


Did you know?


In December, public holidays come on top of weekends. In France and Italy, that means more days when trucks over 7.5 t are not allowed on the road. Good habit: plan your deliveries and breaks now.


France (over 7.5 t, whole road network)

  • From Thursday 24 December 10 pm to Friday 25 December 10 pm
  • From Thursday 31 December 10 pm to Friday 1 January 10 pm
  • And every weekend, from Saturday 10 pm to Sunday 10 pm


Italy (over 7.5 t, outside built-up areas, 9 am to 10 pm)

  • Sundays 6, 13, 20 and 27 December
  • Tuesday 8, Friday 25 and Saturday 26 December


Spain

  • No general ban, but restrictions on certain road sections. Catalonia, the Basque Country and Navarre have their own rules.


Local bans may be added: check before you set off. And on Michelin Truckfly, find HGV parking along your route in advance.


Sources: Bison Futé · Italian Ministry of Transport · DGT
