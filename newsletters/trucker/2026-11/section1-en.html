1. Christmas and New Year: pick the right truck stop


Busy roads, closed restaurants, packed car parks: over the holidays, finding a good stop takes time. Here’s how Michelin Truckfly helps you before, during and after the holidays.


Before

Plan your stops along your route. Look at everything: photos, reviews and now likes. The most liked contributions point you to the most reliable places.


During

Read the most recent reviews to see what’s open (showers, restaurant, shop) and how busy the car park is.


After

Leave a review, add a photo or like a useful contribution: you help the next driver, and every action earns you 1 point.


Heading to the continent? In France, trucks over 7.5 t can’t drive on 25 December and 1 January.
